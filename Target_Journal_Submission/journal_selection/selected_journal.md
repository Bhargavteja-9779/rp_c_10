## Why not the alternatives (summary)
See `journal_comparison.md`. Among ≥76 journals measured, only a handful had a measured median ≤72 days; of these, EP Europace combines (i) OUP publisher (user preference), (ii) the highest JIF among scope-compatible fast candidates (10.2), (iii) verified Q1 rank, and (iv) genuine topical fit for an AF-burden study.
