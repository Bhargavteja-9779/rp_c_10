# Results

## Cohorts
The development pool comprised {{D:dev_total|patients}} patients ({{D:dev_total|hours}} h of analysed RR data; {{D:dev_total|patients_af}} with AF), and the external cohorts {{D:ext_total|patients}} patients ({{D:ext_total|hours}} h; {{D:ext_total|patients_af}} with AF and {{D:ext_total|patients_afl}} with atrial flutter): SHDB-AF {{D:shdb-af|patients}} patients ({{D:shdb-af|patients_af}} with AF during the recording), CPSC2021 {{D:cpsc2021|patients}} patients ({{D:cpsc2021|patients_af}} with AF) and Icentia11k {{D:icentia11k|patients}} patients ({{D:icentia11k|patients_af}} with AF) (Table 1; Supplementary Figure S1). Ten of the {{D:-|icentia_sampled}} sampled Icentia11k patients had no analysable labelled windows.

## Window-level detection
All detectors discriminated AF windows well (Supplementary Table S2, Supplementary Figures S2–S4). For LightGBM, the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve was {{wm:LightGBM|dev_test|auroc}} in the development test set and {{wm:LightGBM|ext_shdb-af|auroc}}, {{wm:LightGBM|ext_cpsc2021|auroc}} and {{wm:LightGBM|ext_icentia11k|auroc}} in SHDB-AF, CPSC2021 and Icentia11k; window sensitivity and specificity were {{wmm:LightGBM|ext_icentia11k|sensitivity}} and {{wmm:LightGBM|ext_icentia11k|specificity}} in Icentia11k.

## Primary pre-specified analysis
With the pre-registered emission model, PA-HMMQ did not reduce burden error compared with classify-and-count in the pooled external cohorts ({{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ|ext_pooled}} vs {{mae:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled}} pp; mean paired difference {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ|ext_pooled|CC|mean_diff}} pp, 95% CI {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ|ext_pooled|CC|mean_ci}}; Holm-adjusted {{P:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ|ext_pooled|CC}}) and was worse than median-filtered counting ({{mae:LightGBM|CC-MF|ext_pooled}} pp) (Table 2; Supplementary Table S14). The primary hypothesis was therefore not supported for the pre-registered specification.

By the development-data rule of Amendment A1, applied before the full analysis, probability emissions were selected (development error {{S:amendment_A1|dev_mae_raw_emission}} vs {{S:amendment_A1|dev_mae_pre_registered}} pp). This final PA-HMMQ had the lowest mean error of all estimators in each external cohort (Table 2, Figure 2): {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_shdb-af}} pp in SHDB-AF, {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_cpsc2021}} pp in CPSC2021 and {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_icentia11k}} pp in Icentia11k, versus {{mae:LightGBM|CC|ext_shdb-af}}, {{mae:LightGBM|CC|ext_cpsc2021}} and {{mae:LightGBM|CC|ext_icentia11k}} pp for classify-and-count. In the development test set—which, unlike the external cohorts, contributed to the A1 selection—it was not better than the pre-registered variant or the fixed-dynamics HMM ({{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|dev_test}}, {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ|dev_test}} and {{mae:LightGBM|HMM-src|dev_test}} pp).

In the pooled external cohorts, the mean paired difference favoured the final PA-HMMQ over every comparator, with bootstrap 95% CIs excluding zero (Table 4; Supplementary Figure S6): {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC|mean_diff}} pp ({{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC|mean_ci}}) versus classify-and-count, {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC-MF|mean_diff}} pp ({{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC-MF|mean_ci}}) versus median-filtered counting and {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|PCC-iso|mean_diff}} pp ({{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|PCC-iso|mean_ci}}) versus isotonic-recalibrated probability averaging. The pre-specified Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, however, were significant after Holm correction against classify-and-count ({{P:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC}}), PCC, PCC-iso, EMQ and HMM-src, but not against median-filtered counting ({{P:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|CC-MF}}), ACC ({{P:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|ACC}}) or PACC ({{P:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|PACC}}). Most external patients were AF-free and received near-zero burden from all estimators, so median paired differences were close to zero; the mean gain was concentrated in patients with AF ({{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|mae_af_present_pp}} vs {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|mae_af_present_pp}} pp) and in AF-free patients with false-positive windows (mean false burden {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|af_free_mean_false_burden_pp}} vs {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|af_free_mean_false_burden_pp}} pp; Table 3). Because counting-type estimators return exactly zero for many AF-free patients whereas HMM posteriors are small but non-zero, rank tests were partly driven by differences below clinical resolution: with estimates reported at 0.1-pp resolution (post hoc), the final PA-HMMQ was better than median-filtered counting (mean paired difference {{testr:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|CC-MF|mean_diff}} pp, Holm-adjusted {{testr:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|CC-MF|p_holm}}), ACC ({{testr:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ACC|p_holm}}) and PACC ({{testr:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|PACC|p_holm}}) (Supplementary Table S17).

The final PA-HMMQ had a lower mean error than classify-and-count in all nine detector × external-cohort combinations. The ranking was reproduced with the other detectors (Supplementary Tables S3–S4): pooled external error {{mae:Classical-LR|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled}} vs {{mae:Classical-LR|CC|ext_pooled}} pp (Classical-LR) and {{mae:RRNet|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled}} vs {{mae:RRNet|CC|ext_pooled}} pp (RRNet). Bias was {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|bias_pp}} pp (classify-and-count {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|bias_pp}} pp) and Lin's concordance correlation coefficient {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|ccc|3}} versus {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|ccc|3}} (Table 3, Figure 3).

## What drives the gain
Re-estimating rhythm dynamics per patient did not contribute: the final PA-HMMQ and the HMM with the same emissions but fixed source dynamics had identical error ({{mae:LightGBM|HMM-src[rawE]|ext_pooled}} pp; mean paired difference {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|HMM-src[rawE] (post hoc, same emissions)|mean_diff}} pp, 95% CI {{test:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|HMM-src[rawE] (post hoc, same emissions)|mean_ci}}), and error was insensitive to the prior strength κ (Supplementary Table S5, Supplementary Figure S5). The improvement was therefore attributable to temporal modelling with sharp emission likelihoods. Consistent with this, a longer median filter recovered most of the gain: with the filter length selected on development patients ({{SM:k}} windows; post hoc), median-filtered counting reached {{SM:ext_ksel}} pp in the pooled external cohorts, versus {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled}} pp for the final PA-HMMQ (mean paired difference {{SM:mean_diff}} pp, 95% CI {{SM:mean_ci}}; Supplementary Table S19). Classical prevalence-correction estimators that ignore temporal dependence (ACC, PACC, EMQ) did not consistently improve on classify-and-count, and recalibrating window probabilities on development patients worsened burden error (PCC-iso {{mae:LightGBM|PCC-iso|ext_pooled}} pp).

## Patient classification
Using the presence of ≥30 s of AF, the final PA-HMMQ increased specificity from {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|present_30s_spec|3}} to {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|present_30s_spec|3}} and decreased sensitivity from {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|present_30s_sens|3}} to {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|present_30s_sens|3}}; on seed-majority classifications it corrected {{mcn:present_30s|a_correct_b_wrong}} patients misclassified by classify-and-count and misclassified {{mcn:present_30s|a_wrong_b_correct}} that it had classified correctly (exact McNemar P {{mcn:present_30s|p}}). The {{NM:n}} patients newly classified as AF-free by the final PA-HMMQ had between {{NM:af_minutes_min}} and {{NM:af_minutes_max}} min of reference AF (maximum burden {{NM:burden_max_pct|2}}%); {{NM:n_ge_6min}} of them had ≥6 min (post hoc; Supplementary Table S16). For a burden of ≥1%, specificity was {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|ge1pct_spec|3}} versus {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|ge1pct_spec|3}} and sensitivity {{bm:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_pooled|ge1pct_sens|3}} versus {{bm:LightGBM|CC|ext_pooled|ge1pct_sens|3}} (Supplementary Table S7).

## Uncertainty intervals
Normalised conformal intervals around the final PA-HMMQ covered the reference burden in {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|dev_test|coverage}} of development test patients and {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|coverage}} (95% CI {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|cp95}}) of external patients ({{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_shdb-af|coverage}} SHDB-AF, {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_cpsc2021|coverage}} CPSC2021, {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_icentia11k|coverage}} Icentia11k); mean width {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|mean_width_pp|2}} pp and median width {{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|median_width_pp|2}} pp (Table 5, Figure 4). Coverage was lower in patients with AF ({{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|coverage_af}}) than in AF-free patients ({{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[rawE]+conformal(norm)|ext_pooled|coverage_af_free}}). Constant-width conformal intervals around classify-and-count reached similar coverage ({{iv:LightGBM|CC+conformal(const)|ext_pooled|coverage}}) but were much wider (mean {{iv:LightGBM|CC+conformal(const)|ext_pooled|mean_width_pp|2}} pp), and uncalibrated posterior credible intervals under-covered ({{iv:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ posterior 90% CrI|ext_pooled|coverage}}). In a post-hoc experiment, recomputing the conformal quantile from 20 randomly chosen, locally labelled patients of each external cohort restored mean coverage to {{LR:ext_shdb-af|20|mean_cov}} in SHDB-AF, {{LR:ext_cpsc2021|20|mean_cov}} in CPSC2021 and {{LR:ext_icentia11k|20|mean_cov}} in Icentia11k, but coverage varied between draws (SHDB-AF 5th percentile {{LR:ext_shdb-af|20|p05_cov}}), remained below nominal in patients with AF ({{LR:ext_shdb-af|20|mean_cov_af}}, {{LR:ext_cpsc2021|20|mean_cov_af}} and {{LR:ext_icentia11k|20|mean_cov_af}}), and widened SHDB-AF intervals to a mean of {{LR:ext_shdb-af|20|mean_width}} pp (Supplementary Table S18).

## Post-hoc emission selection
Selecting the emission sharpness per detector on calibration patients (Revision R1, post hoc) gave a pooled external error of {{mae:LightGBM|PA-HMMQ[sel]|ext_pooled}} pp with LightGBM, {{mae:Classical-LR|PA-HMMQ[sel]|ext_pooled}} pp with Classical-LR and {{mae:RRNet|PA-HMMQ[sel]|ext_pooled}} pp with RRNet, and did not improve on the A1 specification (Supplementary Tables S5, S15).

## Robustness and sensitivity analyses
In SHDB-AF, errors of all estimators increased with simulated false R peaks and premature beats, while missed beats and timing jitter had smaller effects; the ranking of estimators was largely preserved (Figure 5; Supplementary Tables S9–S10). With 25% of the training patients, or with 30- or 120-beat windows, the final PA-HMMQ remained the most accurate of the compared estimators in SHDB-AF and CPSC2021 (the fixed-dynamics HMM with identical emissions was not computed in these runs) (for example, with 25% of training patients {{T2:table11_sensitivity_trainfrac_window|Setting|25% training data|Estimator|PA-HMMQ[rawE]|ext_cpsc2021}} vs {{T2:table11_sensitivity_trainfrac_window|Setting|25% training data|Estimator|CC|ext_cpsc2021}} pp for counting in CPSC2021; Supplementary Table S11).

## Error analysis
Errors were largest in patients with intermediate burden and in patients with atrial flutter (Figure 6; Supplementary Tables S6, S13). Of the {{D:ext_total|patients}} external patients, {{afl:CC|n_with_afl}} had flutter; with an AF+flutter reference the error of the final PA-HMMQ was {{afl:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|mae_afafl}} pp, and excluding flutter patients (post hoc) {{afl:PA-HMMQ[rawE]|mae_no_afl}} pp (classify-and-count {{afl:CC|mae_afafl}} and {{afl:CC|mae_no_afl}} pp; Supplementary Table S8). Absolute error correlated with flutter burden (Spearman ρ = {{S:error_correlations|afl|rho}}) and reference burden (ρ = {{S:error_correlations|true_burden|rho}}), inversely with recording duration (ρ = {{S:error_correlations|hours|rho}}), and only weakly with ectopic beats (supraventricular ρ = {{S:error_correlations|ectopy_sv|rho}}; ventricular ρ = {{S:error_correlations|ectopy_v|rho}}; Supplementary Figure S7). Error did not differ materially by sex or age in SHDB-AF (Supplementary Table S12).
