# Supplementary material

## S1. Pre-registration and design decisions
The full analysis plan (candidate topics considered, stress test, novelty map, hypotheses, research questions, splits, seeds, fixed hyper-parameters, endpoints and statistical tests) was committed to version control before any model was trained and is provided as the file *S1_research_design_preregistration.md*. One amendment (A1) was recorded after a one-seed pipeline check and before the full analysis: the emission model of PA-HMMQ would be selected on development patients only (calibration-B and development test patients, LightGBM, mean per-patient absolute error). The rule selected the probability-emission variant (development error {{S:amendment_A1|dev_mae_raw_emission}} vs {{S:amendment_A1|dev_mae_pre_registered}} pp). The one-seed check also displayed errors for 40-patient subsets of two external cohorts (disclosed in S1). Deviations from the plan: (i) reduced-training-data and window-length analyses omitted Icentia11k for computational reasons; (ii) post-hoc analyses: fair-comparator tests (same emissions, fixed dynamics), per-detector emission selection (R1; selection on calibration-A patients, on which the source dynamics are also fitted, so the selection may be optimistic), exclusion of flutter patients, newly missed patients and tests at 0.1-pp resolution; (iii) one SHDB-AF recording was excluded after the leakage audit (below).

## S2. Data processing and leakage audit
Only beat and rhythm annotation files were downloaded and verified against PhysioNet SHA-256 checksums. CPSC2021 records of patient 51 (11 records) were not retrievable from the repository (HTTP 404) and are absent. In Icentia11k, beats outside labelled rhythm regions were treated as unlabelled and excluded. The automated audit hashed every 60-beat window (RR rounded to 8 ms) and fingerprinted every recording. SHDB-AF Data_ID 020 was an exact RR-level duplicate of Data_ID 005 (same Subject_ID; metadata Holter dates 2021 and 2024) and was removed; after removal no recording-level duplicates remained, and {{L:duplicate_windows_shared_across_patients}} individual windows (of {{L:windows_total}}) were shared between different patients, consistent with chance identity of very regular rhythms. Patient-level splits were verified disjoint for every seed, and no development patient occurred in an external cohort. The SHDB-AF metadata columns are Data_ID/Subject_ID (not the names given in the database README); patients with two recordings were aggregated. MITDB paced records were retained as non-AF recordings. Windows separated by an invalid RR interval within a recording were treated as adjacent in the hidden Markov model (valid intervals constituted ≥99.8% of intervals in every database).

## S3. Detector details
*Features (16).* Mean RR; SDNN; coefficient of variation; RMSSD/mean RR; pNN50; pNN20; median |ΔRR|/mean RR; interquartile range/median RR; turning-point ratio; normalised Shannon entropy of the 16-bin RR histogram; Poincaré SD1/mean RR, SD2/mean RR and SD1/SD2; a coefficient-of-sample-entropy-type irregularity measure, computed as SampEn(m = 2, r = 0.2·SD) + ln(0.06) − ln(mean RR) (a hybrid of sample entropy and the COSEn normalisation, not the original fixed-tolerance COSEn); fraction of sign changes of ΔRR; min/max RR ratio. *Classical-LR* used normalised RMSSD, Shannon entropy and turning-point ratio with standardisation fitted on training patients. *LightGBM*: up to 400 trees, learning rate 0.05, 31 leaves, row and column subsampling 0.8, early stopping (50 rounds) on inner-validation patients. *RRNet*: input two channels (log RR minus window mean; ΔRR), three 1-D convolutional blocks (32 channels, kernel 5, batch normalisation, ReLU, residual connections), bidirectional GRU (32 units per direction), mean and max pooling, two-layer head with dropout 0.2; AdamW (learning rate 10⁻³, weight decay 10⁻⁴), cosine schedule, batch 512, ≤12 epochs, early stopping (patience 3) on inner-validation binary cross-entropy; {{param:RRNet}} parameters. RRNet is a re-implementation inspired by ArNet/ArNet2, not the original code or weights. PyTorch was run with deterministic algorithms; LightGBM used four threads without its deterministic flag, so re-runs may differ negligibly.

## S4. PA-HMMQ and interval details
Hidden states z<sub>t</sub> ∈ {0 (non-AF), 1 (AF)} for consecutive windows of a recording; emissions e<sub>k</sub>(x<sub>t</sub>) are fixed: in the final variant e<sub>1</sub> = p<sub>t</sub>, e<sub>0</sub> = 1 − p<sub>t</sub> (p clipped to [10⁻⁶, 1 − 10⁻⁶]); in the pre-registered variant e<sub>k</sub> is a Laplace-smoothed 50-bin histogram density of logit(p) among calibration-A windows of class k. The source transition matrix and initial distribution are add-one-smoothed frequencies of reference window labels in calibration-A sequences. For each patient i, expectation–maximisation alternates forward–backward smoothing (all recordings of the patient share A<sub>i</sub>, π<sub>i</sub>) with the MAP updates A<sub>i</sub>(k,l) ∝ Σξ(k,l) + κA<sub>src</sub>(k,l) and π<sub>i</sub>(k) ∝ Σγ<sub>1</sub>(k) + κπ<sub>src</sub>(k), until the relative log-likelihood change is <10⁻⁶ (≤50 iterations). Burden is Σd<sub>t</sub>γ<sub>t</sub>(1)/Σd<sub>t</sub>. Convergence is assessed on the summed log-likelihood over all patients of a cohort. EMQ is applied to isotonic-recalibrated posteriors (calibration A) with the duration-weighted source prior. Posterior samples of the whole state path are drawn by forward-filtering backward-sampling at the converged parameters; σ̂<sub>i</sub> is the standard deviation of the duration-weighted sampled burden. EMQ is recovered by removing temporal dependence and HMM-src by fixing A<sub>i</sub> = A<sub>src</sub>.
