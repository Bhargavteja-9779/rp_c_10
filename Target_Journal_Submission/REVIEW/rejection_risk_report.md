## Phase 42 — Final self-decision
**READY FOR SUBMISSION — with mandatory author actions before upload** (complete author list/affiliations, funding, conflicts, repository URL/DOI, confirm the AI-use statement, and personally verify the analyses).

Scientific determination: all experimentally defensible requirements identified in the ten review rounds were addressed with executed experiments (fair comparators, tuned-smoother check, local recalibration, rounded-resolution tests, newly-missed analysis, robustness, sensitivity, efficiency on an idle machine). No further experiment that could be run with the available public data and compute would remove the two dominant risks (limited novelty; post-hoc final specification), which are disclosed rather than fixable. The appropriate scientific claim is therefore modest, and the manuscript states it as such.
